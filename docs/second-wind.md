# Second Wind

Unlocked at level 5. Use the Ability Ledger detail, an eligible quest's Second Wind button, or today's workout button. The review dialog names the completion and extra reward; confirming completes it and spends the weekly use atomically.

Adds one extra base Personal XP reward to the activating player. Co-op partners keep their normal reward and their separate weekly use. Daily counter quests use their per-log base reward, including the final log's remainder. Fate, featured, gear and Campaign modifiers keep their existing behavior.

One use per player per guild-local Monday–Sunday week. Undo reverses the awarded XP through the existing completion audit records; it does not refund the weekly use. The More or Harder workout bonus remains independently claimable.

The other audited abilities remain pending staged implementation.
