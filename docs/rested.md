# Rested

Unlocks at level 20. From Character → Ability Ledger → Rested, log one hour without screens or another intentionally restful activity. One completed hour may be logged per guild-local Monday–Sunday week, per player. Logging is a self-report, not a timer or tracked quest.

Banks +25% base Personal XP on the next quest completion. The indicator stays ready until consumption. Charges do not expire or stack; a pending older charge must be used before another can be banked. Daily counter quests use the per-log base reward. Co-op recipients consume only their own banked bonuses.

Normal quest completion, Second Wind quest completion and Gauntlet completion apply it automatically through additive completion APIs. Standalone workout assignments do not consume Rested; their More or Harder and Second Wind rewards remain separate.

Undo reverses XP using existing completion reward records. It does not refund the weekly rest log or charge.
